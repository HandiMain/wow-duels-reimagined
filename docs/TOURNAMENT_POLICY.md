# Tournament rules and safeguards — living policy

Version 1 — approved decisions, 2026-11-25. Update this policy when the creator approves a change or a new rule; add regression coverage for changed behavior. Policy is intent, not evidence of implementation. See TOURNAMENTS.md and RELEASE_VERIFICATION.md for actual implementation and verification status.

## Approved rules

1. One active hosted tournament per player until cancellation or confirmed completion. Current simulated enforcement is per character GUID; account-wide enforcement is not established.
2. Communities are isolated by client, region, supported ruleset and faction. No manual community switch or foreign tournament entries. Unknown identity fails closed.
3. The original creator permanently owns the specific event. The creator cannot register for, compete in or earn competition rewards from their own tournament. Owner-only start, pre-start edits and cancellation. Never transfer ownership itself.
4. Before actual start, the creator remains controller while offline, including events scheduled days ahead. No coordinator election just because the scheduled time arrives or creator is offline. Online peers may eventually relay discovery and pending registration requests; confirmation requires owner authority. No guaranteed offline availability or independent server. Offline relay and edits are not implemented.
5. After actual start, temporary coordination may migrate to an eligible participant if the creator disconnects. A coordinator may synchronize and advance independently confirmed results, never edit the rules, cancel, transfer ownership, forge results or gain extra privileges in their own matches.
6. When the creator reconnects, synchronize the latest authoritative checkpoint and automatically return coordination of that exact tournament to the creator, with a new authority generation. A stale creator copy cannot overwrite current progress. Reject stale coordinator messages.
7. When there is insufficient reliable evidence, or everyone is disconnected, pause rather than invent progress, remove participants or release ownership. Silence is unknown connectivity, not definitive offline status. Preserve confirmed registration and confirmed results; do not infer a defeat merely from disconnection. Explicit no-show, grace period, withdrawal and dispute decisions require later policy approval.
8. Frozen checked-in roster, deterministic bracket, BO1/3/5 and bilateral matching result evidence. Byes are not wins. No ranking or champion credit from test fixtures. Disputes pause advancement; no provisional champion releases ownership.
9. Separate tournament and ranked systems, reuse shared storage/communication/duel evidence. Simulated tests cannot prove live transport, persistence or distributed authority. Keep live gates closed until verified.

## Implementation choices awaiting further review

Stage 7A uses one injected simulated journal and monotonically increasing control terms. Handoff and handback require identical bracket checkpoint copies from ALL frozen checked-in participants, online observed candidate, and synchronized creator copy for handback. This conservative policy can block progress if even one participant is absent; it is not a final election policy and does not establish distributed consensus. Trusted fixture observation adapter, not a live sender identity or election certificate. Later work must define durable election votes, term fencing across independent peer stores, roster/bracket replication, creator handback during partial connectivity, offline registration, real connectivity observation, completion/reward proof, dispute/no-show resolution and retention. Changes to these choices should be explicit and tested.

## Version 2 — Stage 7B simulated recovery refinement
Fixed majority (floor(frozen checked-in roster size / 2) + 1) replaces unanimity for prototype handoff/handback. Absent participants never shrink the electorate. All supplied copies must match, candidate must be observed online and have exact checkpoint, returning creator must also match. Unknown connectivity cannot establish creator offline. This is a single simulated journal policy, not independent peer consensus. Unconfirmed relayed listings and pending join hints do not confer authority, refresh verified owner sightings or release any commitment. Original verified sender must retry directly with creator; if nobody carrying data is online or origin unavailable, no guaranteed discovery/confirmation. Relay mailbox is bounded volatile fixture data, not disk or live transport. Progress replicas accept only append-only validated bracket logs from current observed coordinator and exact term, not independent election certificates. Diagnostic optimization deferred until tournament feature completion at user request.

Stage 7B integrity refinement: the progress-replica fixture verifier must supply the exact independently accepted journal log in addition to current control. Reject a valid-looking appended duel report invented by the coordinator. Single-journal evidence only; live independent report receipts and certificates still required.

## Version 3 — Stage 7C1 completion and administrative hold prototype
Only the original creator, holding the current unpaused coordination term, may finalize. A complete undisputed replay and exact checkpoint acknowledgements from every checked-in participant are required in this prototype. A provisional champion is not a finalized result. Completion atomically releases host commitment, archives the full simulated evidence and responds to outstanding participant requests with a distinct owner-bound completed receipt. The completed record cannot be cancelled or overwritten; late contradictory evidence after finalization requires a future explicit review/correction policy before live activation. Explicit owner hold preserves roster and reports, increments term; resume uses existing recovery rule. No timeout or disconnect grants a win. Post-start withdrawal adjudication, no-show deadline, rematch/dispute decisions and actual independent peer receipts are still open policy items, not implemented. Completion archive cap 16 full simulated records; reject new publication when full, no silent eviction. Production retention and practical completion quorum are not approved yet.

## Version 4 — reconnect and notification policy
User approved five minutes (300s) of verified offline absence during a ready match with the opponent online. Preserve pre-start registration. Show online/offline/unknown and remaining time. Warn once on countdown start using existing warning notification; notify once on reconnect or forfeit. No repeated sounds per tick. At expiry forfeit the series, not fabricated duel games. Both offline, unknown, pause, observation gap and controller change cannot establish a winner. Voluntary withdrawal only by the affected duelist. Stage 7C2 fixture uses observed states with <=15s sample continuity, resetting on reload or term change; live identity/presence, UI display and actual notification subscription are not implemented. Exact grace retention across reconnecting coordinator and production completion with absent forfeited players remain open. Dispute resolution remains separately planned.

Stage 7C2 completion exception: derive absence exemption only from a replay-validated offline forfeit, never from a coordinator assertion or simple lack of response. All remaining checked-in players including champion must still supply matching copies; conflicting copies always block finalization. Explicit withdrawal alone is not exempt. This is a conservative simulation choice pending multiplayer validation.

## Version 5 — Stage 7C3 unresolved-game replay and final receipts
Proposed simulation defaults: one mutually agreed replay per series, only for an unresolved game. Both original duelists must agree to the exact dispute revision; confirmed games cannot be erased; the replay needs new evidence and attempt identity. Repeated conflict or contradiction of a confirmed result remains held for review/cancellation, never arbitrary victory. Finalization requires matching final duelists' copies (champion always, final opponent unless a canonical forfeit), rather than forcing earlier eliminated players to remain online; all other supplied copies must match. These are single-journal simulations, not live authentication. Late post-final correction and repeat-disconnect policy remain open; no automatic penalties beyond approved continuous five-minute absence.

## Version 6 — tournament gameplay separation (approved intent)
Tournament duels never change normal ranked rating. Reuse the shared detector, result validation, countdown, HUD, sounds and notifications only for their relevant lifecycle; no duplicate detector or permanent tournament polling. Tournament identity must be explicitly agreed before challenge/countdown, tied to event, assigned opponents, current series game, replay attempt and coordinator term. Ordinary duels must never advance the bracket. Stage 8A simulated adapter only; actual live ranked exclusion and lifecycle hookup are mandatory before any live tournament activation. No fake duel records or ranked credit from test fixtures.

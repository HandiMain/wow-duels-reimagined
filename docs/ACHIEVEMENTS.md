No War Games involvement. General milestones require observed standard knockout duels; no retreat/unknown wins; unknown and retreats break streaks. Positive-weight matched evidence only for competitive metrics. Points are derived, never rating or network state. Unlocks/dates are derived deterministically from existing durable canonical history and replay, not a second SavedVariables owner.
